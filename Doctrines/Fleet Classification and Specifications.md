# Introduction
The following meta-doctrine outlines the general methods of classifications and class specifications for ships in service of the Northland Foundation. These specifications are specific to Foundation-service vessels, and as such may differ with other organizations or factions. Any ships or ship classes listed are authorized for general viewing by Foundation staff, and are not to be shared with non Foundation-affiliated individuals or groups.
# Classifications
## Civilian Class
This class covers personal and diplomatic shuttles, cruise ships and other ships that are used in a non-military or industrial role. Ships in this category are lightly armored with durable but slow-recharging shields. In exchange, civilian ships boast comfortable and hospitable environments, with some rivaling entire colonies in luxury. Civilian ships are typically fully crewed and equipped with state-of-the-art medical facilities, personal living quarters, function rooms and observation decks. Some larger ships in this class may also feature luxuries such as indoor pools, ballrooms and even full-sized sports halls. 

The Foundation rarely deploys proper civilian-class ships, only using diplomatic shuttles for negotiations and first-contact encounters with new civilizations. During evacuation operations, cruise ships may be repurposed for bulk passenger transport and used to evacuate citizens.
### Research Class
This is a sub-class of the civilian ship class. Ships of this class are purpose-built for research and expeditionary purposes, and are designed to be self-sustaining over extended duration. Research vessels are less luxurious than true civilian ships, but in exchange have heavier armor, stronger shields and faster engines. Research ships are typically equipped with advanced sensor arrays, research labs and data centers. On larger research ships such as the original and refurbished _Sanctuary_-class expeditionary carriers, they may be outfitted with internal docking bays and maintenance hangars, equipment fabrication bays and supercomputer server rooms.

Due to exorbitant R&D and manufacturing costs, and high risks of piracy by rival factions and organizations, research vessels are typically accompanied by a combat escort fleet. For extended operations measuring in months or years, an auxiliary wing may be deployed to ensure that the research fleet is fully supplied during the duration of the operation.

## Industrial Class
This class covers ships used for industrial operations, such as mining, logistics and construction. These ships are functional in design with minimal armor to avoid obstructions to maintenance and repair. However, they boast some of the strongest shields in the Foundation's fleet, rivaling that of dreadnoughts and carriers. They are armed with anti-missile and anti-fighter defenses such as flak and pulse turrets, defensive drones, as well as traditional and electronic countermeasures. On ships with larger cargo bays, area-of-effect EMP generators and guided missiles may additionally be used to supplement existing defenses. 

Due to the high risk associated with such work, industrial-class ships tend to be fully autonomous. Larger industrial vessels such as the _Rorqual_-class capital mining platform and the _Orca_-class construction ship are lightly crewed, and typically serve the role of supervisors for the thousands of autonomous drones and subsystems on board. Larger industrial ships are sometimes accompanied by a combat escort fleet, but this is a rare occurrence as industrial ships are not frequently targeted. 
### Construction Type
Construction-type ships are specialized ships designed to build, refit and repair stations and outposts. These ships are slow and immobile, and are lightly armored and shielded. They are fitted with point defense turrets to screen against missiles and fighters, and are escorted by a small frigate wing. Construction ships can carry large numbers of specialized builder drones, capable of performing construction work autonomously. Multiple construction ships can be used at once to accelerate station building, with numbers reaching 50 for construction of large projects like the _Prometheus_-class citadel in Sanctuary I. Construction ships have to be fed resources from other transport ships, or can be supplied by a local build storage unit with the necessary materials for the construction project.

## Military Class
This class covers ships used in military settings, including reconnaissance, fleet logistics, command and control, and combat communications. In general, military ships are heavily armored and armed for their size classes, with habitable but rarely hospitable living environments. Depending on the subclass, they may be armed with a wide variety of main weapon batteries, turrets and autonomous drones. 

Due to the high risk associated with such work, almost all military class vessels, with the exception of certain capital ships and all command ships, are unmanned and autonomous. Fleets can be fielded with a human or an AI fleet commander, depending on combat scenario and circumstances.
### Fighters
These are the smallest and most nimble ships in the military class. They have weak shields and armor, in exchange for powerful and maneuverable thrusters and engines. They are fitted with disruption-style weapons designed to disrupt or inhibit a ship's operations. Weapons typically fitted include target lock breakers, sensor dampeners, warp disruption field generators and EMP burst generators. 

Heavy fighters are specialized for anti-capital warfare, typically slower and more durable than regular fighters. They are fitted with high burst damage weapons such as torpedo and bomb launchers, or plasma cannons. Heavy fighters are not suited for dogfighting with small to medium targets, and must be accompanied by escort fighters. Interceptor, or space superiority fighters are extremely agile, paper-thin craft, designed to engage other interceptors and fighters in dogfights and 1-vs-1 battles. These fighters are typically equipped with rapid-fire autocannons and guided interceptor missiles, along with traditional and electronic counter measures. 
### Corvettes
These are the smallest medium-size ships, typically deployed for sector patrol operations as multi-role ships. They are slower and less agile than fighters, but in exchange are more heavily armed and can carry significantly more firepower. Corvettes can also fit a wider range of sensor arrays, making them far more resistant to ECM than fighters. Corvettes typically fill the role of fleet anchor, heavy escort or backup combat communications. Corvettes are the largest ship class that can be piloted by a single captain

In sector quick response fleets (QRFs), corvettes serve as damage dealers, landing on and destroying targets that have already been disabled by interceptor fighters. They can also serve as deployment platforms for deployables such as satellite arrays, resource probes and mines. 

### Frigates
These are medium-sized ships, and the largest ship class that are used in sector QRF operations. They are multi-role vessels, and have a balance between speed and agility, durability and firepower. Frigates are designed for prolonged operations away from logistical support, and can perform reconnaissance and scouting of faraway sectors of space. In QRFs, frigates serve the role of command ship and primary combat communications. In large-scale fleets, frigates can serve a wider range of roles, such as position anchors for fighter wings, forward escorts for auxiliary ships and carriers, and anti-fighter turret platforms. 

### Destroyers
These are large-sized ships, designed to dish out heavy amounts of damage. Destroyers are slow to turn and reposition, with average shields and hull strength. In exchange, destroyers are equipped with powerful main weapon batteries, capable of firing powerful bolts over long distances. They are also equipped with a moderate to large amount of turrets, providing basic anti-fighter and anti-missile capabilities for self-defense. However, destroyer wings are typically recommended to be accompanied by two or more fighter escort wings, as their turrets turn too slowly to defend against fighter swarms. 

In large-scale fleets, destroyers serve the role of anti-structure siege machines, able to bombard stations and other structures while remaining out of station defense range. They can also serve as sub-anchors for larger wings, although this role is rarely assigned outside of very large scale fleets, such as planetary blockades. 

### Carriers
Extra-large-sized ships, carriers are designed to act as command platforms and position anchors in large-scale fleet engagements and operations. These ships are very slow and sluggish without warp or travel engines, and have very tough shields and hull. Additionally, they are equipped with a very large amount of anti-fighter turrets and dedicated fighter launch tubes and maintenance bays. On larger carriers, such as the _Bowhead_-class supercarrier, dedicated medium-sized docking bays are available, allowing for rearm, refuel and resupply operations of corvettes and frigates during extended operations.

Flagship and fleet command carriers are typically assigned with their own personal escort wing, comprising of frigates, corvettes and fighters. Wing command carriers are rarely escorted, as their subordinates are usually within close enough proximity to reinforce the carrier if under attack. Carriers can be resupplied using dedicated auxiliary ships or self-resupply at stations and outposts.

### Battleships
Extra-large-sized ships, battleships are rarely deployed on Foundation operations. These ships are massive, requiring thousands of crew to maintain alongside heavily-automated engineering and maintenance subsystems. Battleships are typically fielded as a show of force, with a massive array of anti-capital and anti-fighter turrets, and powerful main weapon batteries. These ships are completely immobile, and can only be moved using warp engines. Battleships are equipped with small and medium landing pads, allowing for resupply and shuttling of crew between ships. When used in fleet operations, battleships are typically appointed as flagship and fleet commander. Due to their obscene manufacturing costs and strategic value, battleships must be escorted by a dedicated carrier defense fleet. 

Battleships have been nicknamed as "mobile defense stations" because of their large size, massive amounts of firepower and immobility. Ships of this class have only been deployed twice, both on interstellar scale fleet operations. The Foundations' core system, Sanctuary I, and its surrounding systems are reinforced with _Sovereign_-class battleship home defense fleets.

### Auxiliaries
These are typically old or discontinued freighter models, refurbished to function as a mobile supply ship for fleets. Auxiliaries are slow and immobile, and are weakly shielded. In exchange, they have the largest cargo capacities of all military class ships. They are fitted with anti-fighter and anti-missile turrets for self-defense, but will not survive a coordinated bombing run or a corvette swarm. As such, these ships are typically escorted by a dedicated fighter wing, or multiple frigates and corvettes.

The majority of auxiliaries in service to the Foundation are cargo and fuel-ferrying. During peacetime, auxiliaries are used as extensions of the Foundation's logistics arm, utilizing their relatively-strong shields and hull self-repair capabilities to transport goods through sectors with environmental hazards. During evacuation operations, auxiliaries can be refitted for passenger carrying using modular cargo bays, allowing for tens to hundreds of thousands of personnel and civilians to be evacuated at a time.

## Prototech Class
Ships, equipment and modules of this class are marked as experimental in Foundation databases, and have the unique asset ID using the format "PROTO-XXX". Prototech ships are rarely deployed, and can only be piloted by the members of the Board of Directors or Crimson Squadron. Information related to Prototech ships are highly restricted, and only limited information is made accessible to high-ranking Foundation researchers and staff. Prototech ships are unique from each other, with some exceptions:
- All Prototech ships are DNA-locked to a specific captain, which is encoded before an operation
- Prototech ships of all classes are designed to be piloted by a maximum of 5 crew members, no matter the size or complexity
- Prototech ships have specialized cabins that allow it to be piloted by a single skilled captain with appropriate cybernetics

As of time of writing, Prototech ships have only been deployed a total of 2 times, during the Foundation's Defense of Sanctuary I and Operation Great Host Fleet. Both times, Prototech ships acted as force multipliers, and were deciding factors between victory and defeat. Ships of this class have been seen as "divine intervention" due to the rarity of their deployments, their impossibly-high levels of firepower and survivability and the massive volumes of friendly and hostile bloodshed that have occurred as a result of their deployment.
